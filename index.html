Pensamplivox

/pen-samp-liv-ox/

What began as a home for a band of misfits in Wicker Park, Chicago, grew to house many friends from a larger music circle

Pensamplivox Records

Pensamplivox (PVX to its friends) was a scrappy little label out of Wicker Park, Chicago, with a big appetite for loud, strange, gloriously messed-up rock.

Twenty records. Seven artists. Bearhawk and their killer wails of louderness. Century Rocket Building, building rocket centuries. Farm Team, going to rock and taking a left. Friends of Enemies, and whatever’s in the water. GasMasK, harnessing absurdity almost brilliantly under dank skies. Joe Sepi, out on his own. And Lonnie Giles All Star Team, born for a broken man.

It’s all still here. Turn it up and dig in.
